# PotatoS&T 0.11 — 更新公告

**Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1.235 · Java 21**

---

## 中文

**这次从"能发电"走到了"能炼星璨钢"，外加一整套横跨四个维度的装备。**

### 新增机器

- **分馏塔**：塔体 4×4×7 自己搭，配一个控制器（数塔）和一个操作器（开界面）。每 tick 吃 8 mB 原油 + 8096 FE，出柴油 / 石脑油 / 汽油 / 液化石油气，每 5 tick 顺带一块沥青。最多认 4 座塔，需要红石信号才开工。
- **加氢脱硫反应室**：16 沥青 + 1000 mB 氢气 → 硫。**完全不耗电**，靠化学。
- **空气分离器**：原料就是空气，30 秒出 8 mB 氮 + 2 mB 氧。
- **合成氨反应室**：氮 + 氢 + 200 FE → 氨，催化剂槽放铁粉且不消耗。
- **锂电池构造间**：四样原料各一槽，通硫酸，30 秒出一件锂电池元件。
- **酸性反应室**：碳酸 / 硝酸 / 硫酸 / 盐酸，四种酸一台机器。
- **大型柴油发电机**：3×5×2、30 格的多方块。每 tick 烧 1 mB 柴油发 7200 FE。
- **采油机**：站在海洋油田、正下方挂满含水锁链才能开工，井深 n 决定耗电（8n²+80n FE/t）与产量（10n mB/s）。

### 新增内容

- **两条矿物线**：锰、锂（各带深层变种）；原油、柴油、汽油、石脑油、液化石油气、硫酸、盐酸、氨、氯等十余种流体。
- **星仪图之章**：右键切换主世界天空盒，四张星图 + 原版星空循环，第五次回到原版，潜行右键往回切。**只对你自己可见**。
- **星轨坠**：右键召唤陨石，30 秒倒计时（前 10 秒可取消），从 y=200 砸下，7~20 威力带火，落地喷出一批粗矿。全服都能看到倒计时和坐标。
- **振金**：新矿物线 + 一套锻造台升级的装备——无限耐久、弹射物免疫并反弹、爆炸减半、免击退、常驻抗性提升 I、免摔落，受击时 10% 概率原样奉还。
- **星璨钢全套**：剑 / 斧 / 锹 / 镐 / 锄 + 四件装备。夜里采掘与攻击都不磨损耐久；剑可 Shift + 右键斩出 8 格长的**星辉斩**（8×3×3 剑气），斧可放冲击波，头盔给夜视。
- **装备说明瘦身**：套装 tooltip 只留附魔权重，不再罗列耐久与修理材料——那些在 JEI 里。

### 电力

- **银线**上线：同一条 FE 网络上的 **16134 FE/t** 高速档（铜线仍是 2048）。
- 机器不必接线：贴着端子就能换能。端子右键切换 输入 / 输出 / 无。

### 成就

成就树重新补线，共 **43 条**，全部四语言。标题不再只是物品名——「把水拆开」「向大海要盐」「召唤一颗星星」。说明也逐条精简过：**只留这一步该知道的事，配方交给 JEI**。

### 语言

中文 / English / 日本語 / Русский，外加一套完整的 **文言文**（游戏语言菜单里直接选「文言」）。矿名、原石名、装备名都按**原版自己的语言文件**对齐过。

### 还没做完

- **扳手**、**高级金属块**、**稳定金属块**仍只能在创造模式拿到（多方块拆解要靠扳手，这条比较要紧）。
- **钨**目前是死路：黑钨矿能挖、能出粗钨，但还没有东西消耗它。
- 部分贴图仍是原版占位。

## English

**0.11 goes from "you can make power" to "you can smelt Star Steel", plus a full gear line across four dimensions.**

### New machines

- **Fractional Distillation Tower** — build the 4×4×7 tower yourself, add a Controller (counts towers) and an Operator (the GUI). Each tick: 8 mB crude oil + 8096 FE in, diesel / naphtha / gasoline / LPG out, plus one Bitumen every 5 ticks. Up to 4 towers; needs a redstone signal.
- **Hydrodesulfurization Chamber** — 16 Bitumen + 1000 mB hydrogen → Sulfur. **No power at all**; it runs on chemistry.
- **Air Separator** — the raw material is air: 8 mB nitrogen + 2 mB oxygen every 30 s.
- **Ammonia Synthesis Chamber** — nitrogen + hydrogen + 200 FE → ammonia; iron dust in the catalyst slot, never consumed.
- **Lithium Battery Plant** — four inputs, one per slot, fed with sulfuric acid; one Battery Part every 30 s.
- **Acidic Reaction Chamber** — carbonic, nitric, sulfuric and hydrochloric acid: four acids, one machine.
- **Large Diesel Generator** — a 3×5×2 multiblock of 30 blocks; 1 mB of diesel per tick makes 7200 FE.
- **Oil Extractor** — must stand in an Ocean Oilfield with waterlogged chains hanging below; the chain count is your well depth n, setting both the draw (8n²+80n FE/t) and the yield (10n mB/s).

### New content

- **Two ore lines** — manganese and lithium (both with deepslate variants) — plus a dozen fluids: crude oil, diesel, gasoline, naphtha, LPG, sulfuric/hydrochloric acid, ammonia, chlorine and more.
- **Star Chart Tome** — right-click to cycle the Overworld skybox through four star charts and back to vanilla; sneak-right-click goes the other way. **Only you see it.**
- **Starfall Pendant** — right-click to call a meteor: a 30-second countdown (cancellable in the first 10), falling from y=200 at power 7-20 with fire, spraying raw ore on impact. Everyone on the server sees the countdown and the coordinates.
- **Vibranium** — a new ore line and a smithing-table armour set: unbreakable, projectiles reflected, explosions halved, no knockback, permanent Resistance I, no fall damage, and a 10% chance to return any hit exactly as it came.
- **The full Star Steel line** — sword, axe, shovel, pickaxe, hoe and four armour pieces. At night, mining and attacking cost no durability; the sword fires an 8-block **Starlight Slash** on Shift + right-click (an 8×3×3 wave), the axe sends a shockwave, and the helmet grants night vision.
- **Leaner tooltips** — armour sets now show their enchantment weight only; durability and repair materials live in JEI.

### Power

- **Silver wire is in**: a **16,134 FE/t** tier on the same FE network (copper stays at 2,048).
- Machines do not need cables — sitting next to a terminal is enough. Right-click a terminal to cycle input / output / none.

### Advancements

The tree was filled in to **43 advancements**, in all four languages. Titles are no longer just item names — "Split the Water", "Salt From the Sea", "Call Down a Star". Every description was trimmed too: **only what you need at that step; recipes belong to JEI.**

### Languages

English / 中文 / 日本語 / Русский, plus a complete **Literary Chinese** set (pick 文言 straight from the game's language menu). Ore names, raw ore names and gear names were all aligned against **vanilla's own language files**.

### Not done yet

- The **Wrench**, **Advanced Metal Block** and **Stable Metal Block** are still creative-only (the wrench matters most — it is how you take multiblocks apart).
- **Tungsten is a dead end for now**: wolframite drops raw tungsten, but nothing consumes it yet.
- Some textures are still vanilla placeholders.

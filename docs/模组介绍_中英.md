# PotatoS&T —— 模组介绍

**Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · 工业科技 · 中文 / English / 日本語 / Русский / 文言**

---

## 中文

### 这是什么

一个**从矿石到发电、再到一整套装备**的工业模组。

挖矿、粉碎、烧炼、压板、配合金、接电缆、发上电——这条线走得通，而且不折腾：没有需要研究的谜题，也没有几十个小时的等待。你的第一台机器是一台**微型粉碎机**，最后一样东西是**星璨钢**，中间是一条能自己站住的产业链。

### 两条资源

- **FE**（NeoForge Energy）：常规电能。发电机、太阳能板、柴油发电机都产它，机器都吃它。
- **动力**：原版机器"跑起来"就是动力。**动力能源捕获器**盯着紧邻的六个面，流水每格 +8、熔炉与烟熏炉 +8、高炉 +16，再用**发电机**把动力换成电（每点 2 FE/t）。

### 你会做的事

1. **起步**：粉碎机把矿石与锭磨成粉，液压机把锭压成板——板是几乎所有机器的通用零件。
2. **发电**：低级发电机烧煤或木炭；之后是太阳能板（白天发电，越靠正午越强，雨天 60%、雷暴 20%）、再到大型柴油发电机（3×5×2 多方块，每 tick 1 mB 柴油换 7200 FE）。
3. **接线**：端子就是电线，铜线轴 2048 FE/t、银线轴 16134 FE/t。机器贴着端子就能换能，不必拉线。
4. **上高炉**：用 3×3×3 外壳围住一台原版高炉（或本模组的控制器）成型，效率 12 入 32 出。**铁粉 + 碳粉 = 高碳钢**，从这里开始一切都不一样。
5. **上化学**：电解水得氧与氢；海盐进电解槽改产氯；空气分离器取氮；三者凑出氨。氨 + 氧 → 硝酸，硫 + 水 → 硫酸——四种酸一台**酸性反应室**。
6. **上油**：拎着空桶找一处地表油田舀一桶原油，然后采油机 + 分馏塔，把原油拆成柴油、汽油、石脑油、液化石油气和沥青。
7. **上合金**：**合金炉**一台机器搞定轻质钛合金、硬质钛合金、星璨钢与振金——但塔要自己按图纸搭。
8. **收尾**：星璨钢全套（夜里不磨损、剑斧各有招式、头盔给夜视）与**振金套**（无限耐久、弹射物反弹、免击退、免摔落，受击 10% 原样奉还）。

### 有什么不一样

- **不塞流水账**：机器说明只写这一步该知道的事，配方交给 JEI。图纸、机制、告警留着，配方表删掉。
- **成就当引导**：43 条，一条一条告诉你下一步该干什么，不是"你捡到了 X"。
- **五套语言**：中文、English、日本語、Русский，还有一整套**文言文**（游戏语言菜单里直接选「文言」）。
- **两种光照**：JEI 里每台机器的配方都标了时间与耗电；Jade 显示能量缓存。

### 现在还没有

- **扳手**、**高级金属块**、**稳定金属块**只能创造模式拿到（扳手最要紧——拆多方块靠它）。
- **钨**是死路：黑钨矿能挖、能出粗钨，但暂时没人要。
- 部分贴图仍是原版占位。

## English

### What it is

An industrial tech mod that runs **from ore to power to a full set of gear**.

Mine, crush, smelt, press, alloy, wire it up, switch it on — the line works, and it does not waste your time: no research puzzles, no dozens of hours of waiting. Your first machine is a **Micro Crusher**; the last thing you make is **Star Steel**, and in between sits an industry that stands on its own.

### Two resources

- **FE** (NeoForge Energy) — ordinary electricity. Generators, solar panels and the diesel generator all make it, and every machine eats it.
- **Power** — kinetic energy, captured from vanilla machines that are already running. The **Power Capturer** watches all six neighbouring faces: flowing water is +8 per block, a furnace or smoker +8, a blast furnace +16; a **Generator** turns that into electricity at 2 FE/t per point.

### What you will actually do

1. **Start up.** The crusher grinds ore and ingots into dust; the hydraulic press turns ingots into plates — plates are the common part of nearly every machine.
2. **Make power.** A low-tier generator burns coal or charcoal; then solar panels (daylight only, strongest at noon, 60% in rain, 20% in a thunderstorm); then the large diesel generator (a 3×5×2 multiblock, 1 mB of diesel per tick for 7200 FE).
3. **Wire it.** Terminals are the wires: copper spools carry 2,048 FE/t, silver spools 16,134 FE/t. Machines exchange energy just by sitting next to a terminal — no cables required.
4. **Raise the blast furnace.** Wall a vanilla blast furnace (or this mod's own controller) in a 3×3×3 shell: 12 inputs, 32 outputs. **Iron dust + carbon dust = high carbon steel**, and from there nothing is the same.
5. **Do some chemistry.** Electrolyse water for oxygen and hydrogen; drop sea salt in the electrolyte slot and it makes chlorine instead; the air separator gives nitrogen; those three give you ammonia. Ammonia plus oxygen makes nitric acid, sulfur plus water makes sulfuric acid — four acids, one **Acidic Reaction Chamber**.
6. **Go get oil.** Take an empty bucket to a surface oilfield for your first crude, then an Oil Extractor and a Distillation Tower split it into diesel, gasoline, naphtha, LPG and bitumen.
7. **Alloy it.** One **Alloy Smelter** handles light titanium alloy, hard titanium alloy, Star Steel and Vibranium — but you build the tower yourself, from the blueprint in its tooltip.
8. **Finish the set.** The full Star Steel line (no wear at night, a Shift + right-click move on the sword and axe, night vision from the helmet) and the **Vibranium set** (unbreakable, projectiles reflected, no knockback, no fall damage, and a 10% chance to return any hit exactly as it came).

### What is different here

- **No filler text.** Machine tooltips tell you what you need at that step; recipes belong to JEI. Blueprints, mechanics and warnings stay; recipe tables and repeated numbers are gone.
- **Advancements as a guide.** 43 of them, each telling you what to do next rather than announcing what you just picked up.
- **Five languages.** English, 中文, 日本語, Русский — plus a complete **Literary Chinese** set, selectable as 文言 straight from the game's language menu.
- **Both kinds of light.** JEI prints the time and energy on every machine recipe; Jade shows the energy buffers.

### Not there yet

- The **Wrench**, **Advanced Metal Block** and **Stable Metal Block** are creative-only for now (the wrench matters most — it is how you take multiblocks apart).
- **Tungsten is a dead end**: wolframite drops raw tungsten, and nothing wants it yet.
- A few textures are still vanilla placeholders.
